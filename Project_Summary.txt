HR Analytics Dashboard - Project Summary

Title:
HR Analytics Dashboard: Employee Attrition, Salary & Workforce Insights

Objective:
Analyze employee data to identify workforce trends, monitor attrition, evaluate salary distribution, measure department performance and assess workforce demographics.

Tools:
Power BI, Excel/CSV, DAX, SQL, GitHub

Dataset:
30 employee records.

KPIs:
Total Employees = 30
Employees Left = 9
Active Employees = 21
Attrition Rate = 30%

Portfolio Skills Demonstrated:
Data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, KPI design, data visualization, exploratory analysis, SQL and business insight communication.
